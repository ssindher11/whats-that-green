Common Name

Monstera Deliciosa

Monstera deliciosa Liebm.

Botanical Overview

An iconic evergreen vine native to the tropical rainforests of Central America, renowned for its distinctive fenestrated leaves. In indoor spaces, it thrives with moderate care and adds a majestic jungle aesthetic to any room.

Essential Care Guidelines

Water • Light • Soil
Watering Routine

Water when the top 2 inches of soil feel dry to the touch. Avoid overwatering to prevent root rot.

Sunlight Exposure

Thrives in bright, indirect sunlight. Direct harsh afternoon sun can scorch the delicate foliage.

Soil & Potting Medium

Well-draining, peat-based potting mix with perlite or orchid bark to ensure proper aeration.
